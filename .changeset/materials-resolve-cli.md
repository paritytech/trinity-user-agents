`truapi-host materials plan <face.json>` prints the plan a phone draws a card face's materials from: each `Materials` node loading, ready with its shaders, or drawn plain and why. `--materials <dir>` keeps local material files first, `--metal` builds iOS shaders instead of Android's, and `--out <dir>` writes the shaders.
