`truapi-host materials resolve <face.json>` prints what a card face's materials resolve to, with the same core a phone uses: each `Materials` node loading, ready with its shaders, or drawn plain and why. `--materials <dir>` keeps local material files first, `--metal` builds iOS shaders instead of Android's, and `--out <dir>` writes the shaders.
