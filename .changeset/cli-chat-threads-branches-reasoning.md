The `truapi-host dev` chat page lists `threads`, `branches`, `reasoning_rows` and `read_aloud`: thread rooms leave the
list and open from the root's chip with the root quoted, deleting a room deletes its threads, branch arrows send
`BranchSelected` and revert after 5 s without an answer, reasoning rows stream as "Thinking" and collapse to "Thought
for N s", and the message menu reads a message aloud where the room declares it.
