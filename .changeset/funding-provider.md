New `FundingProvider` trait for a provider's worker. The core calls the worker, which answers each call once: `onAssign` hands it a session, again with its last update when the worker connects, `onCancel` asks it to stop one (`Stopped`, or `Offer`/`Refused` once money moved) and `onSettleCancel` passes on the user's answer to its offer. The worker calls the core: `report` stores its progress on the session, and `presentFrame` shows one of its App's screens, or an external https page with an optional return URL, in a host frame. `payment.topUp` and `payment.request` take an optional `funding` intent: a provider's worker sets it to start funds for a session it serves, or the call fails with `FundingNotServed`. The core records them before the host is asked, refuses a repeat under the id with other terms or an id the host already holds with `AlreadyExists`, and `Crediting` and `Collecting` must name funds of that session the host took, at their amount, once, or fail with `NotStarted`. Sessions end as `Delivered` or `Released` from the recorded amounts of the named top-ups once their claims are final, or of the named payment once it completes. An assigned session whose provider has not seen the user's payment expires 7 days after the provider's last update (`FundingAssignment.expiresAt`, `undefined` once funds move), and a second cancel ends it. Hosts assign a session with `select_funding_provider`, and must implement `present_provider_frame` on `FundingPlatform` (natively `NativeFundingCallbacks`). Funding sessions stored by 0.26.0 are dropped on the first load.
