New `FundingProvider` trait for a provider's worker: `serveSubscribe` receives the sessions assigned to it and cancel requests, `report` stores its progress on the session, and `presentFrame` shows one of its App's screens, or an external https page with an optional return URL, in a host frame. The core records the top-ups and payment requests a provider's worker starts while serving a session; `Crediting` and `Collecting` must name one at its amount, once, or fail with `NotStarted`. Sessions end as `Delivered` or `Released` from the recorded amounts of the named top-ups once their claims are final, or of the named payment once it completes. An assigned session with no funds moving expires 7 days after the provider's last update (`FundingAssignment.expiresAt`, `undefined` once funds move), and a second cancel ends it. Hosts assign a session with `select_funding_provider`, and must implement `present_provider_frame` on `FundingPlatform` (natively `NativeFundingCallbacks`).
