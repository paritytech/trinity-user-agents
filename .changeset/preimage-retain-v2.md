`Preimage.retain` v0.2 keeps several items under one confirmation. An item is a preimage key, a CID that Bulletin has,
or a DotNS name that the host resolves to the CID of its contenthash, so a product can keep a whole `bulletin-deploy`
site: the provider keeps every block of its DAG. A dry run answers the provider, the size, the price and how the price
splits between the free allowance and the paid credits, and asks for nothing. An item with `renew` is a lease that the
provider charges one day at a time until it ends or the payer stops it. The answer gives the outcome of each item,
`Fetching` while the provider fetches a large item in the background. A v0.1 request is one key item, and a v0.1 caller
gets the outcome of that item.
