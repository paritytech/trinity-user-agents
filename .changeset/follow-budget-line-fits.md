The follow budget marker names every follow holding a slot, each with how long it has been open, when it last saw an event, how many calls it has taken and when it last took one, and saying `dropped` where its receiver has gone. Nothing is cut when the line fits the room the host sink forwards. When it does not, the longest entries share what is left and lose the end of their names, marked, while every follow keeps its activity, so the line reaches a host whole and still says which follows are stale or gone.
