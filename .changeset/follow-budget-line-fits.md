The follow budget marker names every follow holding a slot, each with how long it has been open, when it last saw an event, how many calls it has taken and when it last took one, and saying `dropped` where its receiver has gone. Each follow has an equal share of the room the host sink forwards and an entry longer than its share is cut to it, marked, with the name ahead of the activity, so the line reaches a host whole and a follow can still be chased to its product.
