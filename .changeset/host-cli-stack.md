`truapi-host` runs against any Trinity stack with `--stack <descriptor URL | stack link | file>` (or `HOST_CLI_STACK`), for example `truapi-host --stack 'polkadotapp://stack?key=0x…&url=…' signing-host`, so forks and staging instances whose genesis differs from a built-in network work without a new release. The stack supplies the People, Asset Hub and Bulletin endpoints and genesis hashes, the identity backend from its `dub` service, the TLD and the dotNS `popController`. `--stack` and `--network` cannot be combined; a stack's product namespace is its descriptor's `id` without a trailing `-fork`, except that a `polkadot-fork` stack keeps its full `id`. A stack with the id `polkadot` is refused, and so is `--chain-backend light-client` with `--stack`. A fork of Polkadot may keep Polkadot's genesis hashes and the `dot` TLD; an endpoint with one of Polkadot's genesis hashes stops the command when its block hash at the lower of its and Polkadot's finalized block equals Polkadot's, and when it, or every Polkadot node, does not answer that comparison. `--network` is now a global option, and `paseo-next-v2` and `previewnet` are now built-in stacks: the CLI reads the descriptor their operators publish, falls back to the signed copy the release ships when it is unreachable, and prints a line when the published one changes a field the CLI reads. Before a command runs over RPC on a built-in stack or a `--stack`, every endpoint that answers within 3 seconds must report the descriptor's genesis, and People must report the stack's `tld` as its `NetworkSuffix`; a built-in stack whose chains restarted fetches its descriptor once more when it was unreachable, and otherwise fails with an error that reports which descriptor is in use. `truapi-host stack bundle` refuses a copy that changes the `tld` of the one at `--out`, and with `--skip-same-projection` leaves that copy in place when only fields the CLI does not read changed. `HOST_CLI_PEOPLE_WS`, `HOST_CLI_ASSET_HUB_WS` and `HOST_CLI_BULLETIN_WS` now also reroute a chain of a `--stack`. `HOST_CLI_LOCAL_NETWORK_CONFIG` cannot be combined with `--stack` and reads the built-in network's bundled copy without fetching its descriptor. `network-check` prints the routing of any network, stack or override as JSON without contacting a chain.
