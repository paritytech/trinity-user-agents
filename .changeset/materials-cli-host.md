With `--execution-kind worker` and `TRUAPI_POCKET_CARDS`, the `truapi-host` signing host answers yes to the materials question. It opens a render stream for each card as the product connects, resolves the materials of every face the product draws as a phone does, reports each fallback through `Renderer::failureSubscribe`, and records each result in `TRUAPI_POCKET_LOG`.
