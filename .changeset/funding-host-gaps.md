Funding for the host's screens: a crypto `FundingQuoteAsk` names its `network`, Worker manifest crypto routes declare `networks`, and candidates carry the `limits` quote refusals showed. `funding_progress` reports `retrying` and an outbound session's `payout`, which the provider reports after release with the new `FundingUpdate.Payout`. `FundingFailure.Refunded` ends an inbound payment the provider returned.
