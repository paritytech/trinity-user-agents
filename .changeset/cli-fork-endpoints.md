The CLI host takes `HOST_CLI_PEOPLE_WS`, `HOST_CLI_ASSET_HUB_WS` and `HOST_CLI_BULLETIN_WS` to point a network preset at a fork of that network, and `truapi-host attest` attests a given mnemonic's identity, waits for its ring membership, and prints `{address, publicKey, liteUsername}` as JSON.
