Fundtest

A reference funding provider for the Paseo Next v2 testnet.

Fundtest pretends to take card and bank payments, then moves real testnet funds into your CASH balance through Asset Hub and People. Use it from the CASH card's Add funds or Withdraw, and pick Fundtest as the provider.

Amounts ending in .13 are refunded, .23 credit in two parts, and .33 arrive late. Nothing here touches real money.
